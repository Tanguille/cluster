## Decision

Use a printed grille insert in the stock plastic door, with a front overlap lip and screw-adjusted rear clamp tabs. Retain the stock perimeter, hinge retainers and closure hardware. Use one tall window inside the retained border, with the least obstruction across the lower fan region. The insert moves with the door to expose the optical bays.

The requested 15–20 mm retained border is a starting constraint, not a verified safe cut line. Preserve complete moulded hardware pockets and screw bosses even if the border needs local widening. Remove sound damping within the opening and clamp contact lands; clamps must bear on plastic, not compressible padding.

Choose black PETG, a shallow hexagonal grille, and two vertically stacked printed sections if the final length exceeds the bed. Place the horizontal seam in a deliberate grille rib near the division between fans; use an in-plane dovetail and adhesive. Print the flat front face down. Final part bounds must include the joint and any brim. PETG is a reasonable choice for toughness and temperature resistance; see [Prusa's material guidance](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/petg_2059).

This is a proposed design, not a fit-certified part. Retaining the hinges avoids reproducing their fit but does not eliminate interference or weakened-frame risk.

## What the evidence establishes

- **Construction:** plastic door with an aluminium-look finish and bonded sound-damping backing. It is not a steel or aluminium sheet door. Bit-tech describes some flex; sources call the damping foam or dense insulation, so its exact chemistry is not assumed. [Bit-tech review](https://bit-tech.net/reviews/tech/cases/fractal-design-define-r5-review/1/), [Tech-Review](https://www.tech-review.de/test-fractal-design-define-r5.html).
- **Thickness:** Fractal Design NA's Sales and Community Manager describes the door as approximately a quarter inch thick. This is about 6.4 mm after conversion, not a manufacturing tolerance or confirmation of the central plastic skin thickness. [Manufacturer representative, post 7](https://hardforum.com/threads/anyone-have-experience-with-diypc-cases.1904204/post-1042404627).
- **Closing:** the R5 physically latches/clips into the bezel; it does not use the older magnetic closure. There are no supported stock magnet locations to design around. [Firsthand OCAU review](https://forums.overclockers.com.au/threads/fractal-designs-define-r5-watercoolers-dream.1153575/), [APH review](https://aphnetworks.com/reviews/fractal-design-define-r5/2).
- **Hinges:** reversible, with two screw-retained hinge arrangements along the selected vertical side. Preserve upper/lower hardware zones on both sides to retain reversibility. The official reversal diagram shows the retainers and screws but does not dimension their coordinates or pins. [Fractal reversal instructions](https://support.fractal-design.com/support/solutions/articles/4000055500-define-r5-change-opening-direction-of-the-front-door), [review hinge close-up](https://www.tech-review.de/assets/pool/tests/gehaeuse/Fractal_Design_Define_R5/12_Fractal_Design_Define_R5.jpg), [inside-door photograph](https://www.tech-review.de/assets/pool/tests/gehaeuse/Fractal_Design_Define_R5/11_Fractal_Design_Define_R5.jpg).
- **Drive/fan layout:** two optical bays are stacked at the top; the removable front intake filter is below them. The front supports two 140 mm fans. Factory supply is one front GP-14 and one rear GP-14; the user's two front GP-14s are the design configuration. [Fractal product page](https://www.fractal-design.com/products/cases/define-series/define-r5/define-r5-black/).
- **Opening:** one physical review reports a 95° stock opening. Preserve the stock travel, then test actual DVD ejection with a clearance coupon. Exact tray dimensions depend on the user's drive and are not documented here. [Linuxlookup review](https://www.linuxlookup.com/review/fractal_design_define_r5_window_case_review).

The official illustrated manual was inspected via a [public mirror](https://gzhls.at/blob/ldb/6/e/7/b/204ea90228a6ac518d84ae2667b28c66a0c4.pdf). Its optical-drive, hinge-reversal and filter diagrams establish arrangement, not a dimensioned manufacturing drawing. The [official manual landing page](https://support.fractal-design.com/support/solutions/articles/4000038729-define-r5-product-manual) remains the canonical reference.

## Airflow acceptance

The user's observation is a 7.7°C Tctl reduction at the same load with the door open. It supports opening the direct intake path; it does not specify airflow in volumetric units.

Target roughly 80–85% open area in the repeated grille pattern. Compute net clear projected area for the insert, the whole modified door, and the lower fan-facing region separately, including the retained border, seam, mounting intrusions and clipped boundary cells where applicable. Aim to meet 60% even over the whole modified door; do not claim that from the pattern percentage alone. The lower fan region must independently meet at least 60%, so openings over the DVD bays cannot compensate for a restrictive intake. Whether the retained hardware lands allow these targets is still a CAD gate. Exact cell pitch and web dimensions remain engineering choices for modelling, explicitly separate from sourced stock dimensions.

An added grille still has resistance. Equal open area does not establish equal pressure drop or open-door cooling. Final acceptance needs a repeated comparison of modified-door closed versus open with the same stock filter, fan settings, workload and comparable ambient conditions. Use already available telemetry; no dimensional measuring is requested. A proposed practical target is within 1°C of the repeat open-door result, subject to normal run-to-run variation; this is a proposed acceptance threshold, not a predicted result.

## Coupon-first plan

Small dimensions below are **proposed coupon geometry**, not claims about the R5. They derive from the user's trial-fit/0.2 mm increment requirement and print-time budget. Public sources do not specify custom coupon dimensions. All stock mating dimensions remain in the source register above.

Use the same black PETG, nozzle, orientation and slicer settings as the final parts. The 16 released variants fit inside 44 × 44.456 × 6.2 mm maximum extents and were sliced individually on the stock Centauri Carbon 0.4 mm/PETG profile. Estimates are 4m18s–11m25s; each is below a 20-minute target that leaves startup margin. Firmware macro/heating duration is unknown, so a total cold-start time below 30 minutes is not yet verified. The [coupon guide](define-r5-coupons/README.md) now starts with **B6p2, D_Male and D0p4**: three pieces, 21m46s summed slicer estimates. Other variants are conditional, A and G optional, and C deferred until representative material is accessible.

| Coupon | Variants / method | User's fit-only check | Release condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: optional border and corner locator | Edge-registering samples for 15, 17.5 and 20 mm retained border; thin face samples held in place | Selected label seats against the reference edge and clears moulded bosses and closure hardware | A later complete paper fit can replace this local placement check; paper cannot validate lip thickness or swing clearance |
| B: rear-clearance witness | Matches the 28 × 12 mm rear-tab footprint; 2.4 mm coarse witness and 5.4–6.2 mm nominal nut/thread-stack projections in 0.2 mm steps | Door closes gently and latches normally; no rubbing on filter, drive face, bezel or hinges; DVD ejects with door open | Test intended locations, then retain clearance below the largest passing projection; actual tilted clamp envelope remains a later check; never force the latch |
| C: clamp section | One short lip plus screw-adjusted backing tab; broad adjustment rather than a thickness-specific snap fit | Clamp holds bare plastic without crushing, cracking or projecting into the filter | Confirm at an accessible representative section, or on the offcut after the cut is separately approved; outer moulded edge thickness must not be assumed equal to the central skin |
| D: seam sample; G: separate grille sample | Dovetail halves with 0.2, 0.4 and 0.6 mm total width-clearance variants; G has 20 mm hex openings on 22 mm pitch, 2.4 mm thickness | Select snug hand-assembled joint; face remains flush; G webs survive normal handling | Confirm before full-size parts; no press fit relying on layer separation; G's 70.84% net openness does not establish full-door openness |

No hinge-pin coupon is needed for the selected approach. If it becomes necessary to replace the door, first obtain a credible public hinge nominal and test isolated pin/retainer variants in 0.2 mm increments plus an alignment coupon. Do not guess replacement hinges from the case dimensions.

Before cutting, the complete outline still needs a 1:1 paper dry fit with corner/edge references and all protected zones marked. Match a D coupon to its printed silhouette in both directions to catch paper scaling mistakes without a ruler, then check the complete paper outline on the door. The template is **not** available yet. The selected window size is a design choice; suitably referenced public photos or the candidate Fusion envelope can seed a provisional paper layout, with uncertainty documented. Only use another rigid span/position coupon if paper cannot register reliably. No irreversible cut based solely on the approximate 6.4 mm figure, case envelope or unassigned Fusion expressions.
